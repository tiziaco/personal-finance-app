## 1. Scope

Two portals over one API. Internal project managers manage suppliers, orders and document
compliance, with reviewers and approvers beside them; external suppliers upload the
documents each order requires and track where they stand. Every customer is an
organization, and sees only its own data. A fork rewrites this paragraph first.
